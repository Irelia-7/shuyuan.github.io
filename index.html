I am a PhD Candidate in Electronic Engineering at Tsinghua University. I conduct research at the intersection of computer architecture, integrated circuits, and embodied AI, with the goal of enabling efficient perception-control closed-loop computing for edge robots and physical AI systems.

I received a B.S. degree in Electronic Engineering from Tsinghua University in 2023. I am pursuing a Ph.D. in Electronic Engineering at Tsinghua University, advised by Hongyang Jia.
